This policy covers the Vice City Daily website, the Day One Ready launch-alert email list, and Clip Uploader, a private tool that publishes videos made by Vice City Daily to the channel owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

Who uses Clip Uploader

Clip Uploader is used only by the Vice City Daily channel owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload). It does not read your email, contacts, watch history, or any other data.
  • TikTok: basic profile info (display name and avatar, user.info.basic) and permission to publish videos (video.publish) to the owner's account.

How data is used and stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • No analytics or tracking cookies are used on this website. The sign-up form sends your details only to our email provider when you submit it.

Day One Ready email list

  • What we collect: your email address, the platform you play on, your graphics card if you choose to give it, whether you're interested in the PC Launch Kit, and which page or video sent you to the sign-up form.
  • Why: to email you about game launches, official specs, setup advice and, occasionally, gear recommendations (some may be affiliate links, always labelled). We use your platform and graphics card only to send you relevant information.
  • Who processes it: our email provider stores the list on our behalf. We never sell or rent your details and don't share them with anyone else.
  • Your choices: every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. To have your details deleted, email us at the address below.
  • The list is for people aged 18 and over.

Google API Services

Clip Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Clip Uploader uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page or in TikTok under Settings → Security → Apps and services. Revoking access stops all uploads; stored tokens become useless and are deleted from the owner's computer.
