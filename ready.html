PS5 full? Free up space or add more

Quick wins first, no cost:

  1. Go to Settings → Storage → Console Storage to see what's using space.
  2. Delete games you've finished. Your saves live separately and aren't lost.
  3. Have a USB drive? Move PS4 games to it, since they can play from there. PS5 games can be stored on USB to save re-downloading, but they have to be on the internal drive or an M.2 drive to play.

Adding an M.2 SSD: what Sony says it needs

  • PCIe Gen4 x4 M.2 NVMe SSD, 250 GB to 8 TB.
  • Sequential read speed of 5,500 MB/s or faster is recommended.
  • Must have a heatsink or cooling structure (built in, or added separately).
  • Sizes 2230, 2242, 2260, 2280 or 22110 fit.

Always check Sony's official PS5 M.2 SSD page before buying. Xbox Series X|S uses official Storage Expansion Cards instead; a USB drive can store Series X|S games but not play them.
